## Why the Sprint 1 code comes from one person

The first version of the product (PRs #1–#14) was written by Christoph in the first days of
Sprint 1, before the team had met to split the work; Javi reviewed and merged it. The individual
contribution statements show this split. From now on, every member owns at least one
backlog item (table above), and every change still goes through a pull request approved by
another member.
